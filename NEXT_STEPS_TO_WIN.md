# Next Steps To Maximize Winning Odds

No one can guarantee a hackathon win. This is the shortest path to make ChamaScore look like a prize-level submission.

## Must Do Before Submission

1. Push a public GitHub repository.
2. Deploy the app to a public URL.
3. Record a demo video under 3 minutes using `DEMO_SCRIPT.md`.
4. Post the X/Twitter registration post from `SOCIAL_POST.md`.
5. Join the hackathon Telegram: `https://t.me/realworldagentshackathon`
6. Register on ERC-8004 / 8004scan if possible.
7. Complete Self Agent ID, or capture the unsupported-region screenshot if blocked.
8. Fill every `TODO` in `SUBMISSION.md`.

## Do Not Do

- Do not create more demo circles unless needed; Circle `2` is the clean demo.
- Do not claim mainnet production readiness.
- Do not claim real users unless you have them.
- Do not say funds are safe for production custody.
- Do not over-focus on the UI; judges need proof of agent behavior and onchain activity.

## Best Prize Strategy

Primary target:

- `best-agent`

Secondary targets:

- `8004scan-rank`, only if the agent is registered and visible.

Skip for now:

- `most-activity`, unless you can generate many legitimate repeated transactions from real workflow actions. Do not farm fake activity.

Best positioning:

```text
ChamaScore Agent is the trust layer for Celo savings-circle payments: it observes stablecoin contribution behavior, scores reliability, flags risk, and converts payment history into portable reputation.
```

## Final Quality Bar

The project should be judged as:

- Useful to real people.
- Actually onchain.
- Easy to demo.
- Honest about limits.
- Strongly aligned with Celo payments.
- Agentic beyond a simple form or dashboard.
